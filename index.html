What we do

🧠

LLMOps

Model serving, routing, and versioning. vLLM, llama.cpp, SGLang — with caching, quantization, and cost controls that survive real traffic.

🏗️

AI platform engineering

The internal platform your AI teams build on: GPU scheduling, multi-tenant namespaces, quotas, and self-serve deploys.

📚

RAG & retrieval

Ingestion, chunking, embeddings, vector search, reranking. Grounded answers with citations — evaluated, not vibes.

🤖

Agent runtimes

Tool-calling agents in production: sandboxed execution, state, memory, retries, and human-in-the-loop gates.

📊

Evaluation & observability

Golden sets, regression suites, token and latency tracing, drift alerts. Know whether your model change helped or hurt.

🛠️

Cloud & GPU infrastructure

Kubernetes GPU clusters on AWS, GCP, or bare metal. IaC with Terraform and Pulumi — reproducible and audited.
